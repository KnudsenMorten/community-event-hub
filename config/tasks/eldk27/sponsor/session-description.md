Everything we need to publish your session in the programme — filled in once, straight into our system.

:::section About the session
* **Title** and a short **description** (this is what attendees read when choosing sessions)
* **Track**, so we place it in the right part of the programme
:::

:::section About each speaker (up to three)
* **First and last name**, and their **e-mail**
* **LinkedIn URL**
* A **photo** — landscape or square, at least 800px wide
:::

:::section How to send it
Use the **Update Session Details** button on this task. You can save and come back — it remembers what you entered, and you can change anything up to the deadline.
:::
