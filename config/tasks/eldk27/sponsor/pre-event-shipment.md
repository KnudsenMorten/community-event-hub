If you need to ship packages to your booth, please check the venue's rules first: **many venues have no on-site storage** and work with an appointed freight partner that handles storage, handling and delivery directly to your booth in the Expo area.

Ordering is done through our sponsor webshop portal, where you can select the services you need — pre-event and post-event handling, storage, customs clearance, and so on.

:::callout warning
==Please be aware that the pricing for these services can be fairly high.== It is set by the venue and its freight partner and is **out of our hands** as organizers. You are of course free to use any other local shipping or storage company of your choice if you prefer.
:::

:::section For shipments outside the EU
We require the following documents for customs clearance:

* **3 originals** of proforma invoice / packing list in English, showing weights, sizes, values, description of content and Harmonized Codes
* Separate invoices for temporary goods (Exhibits / Standfittings) and final importation (consumables / advertising materials) or send temporary goods by ATA Carnet
* If necessary: Certificate of Origin (Form A / EUR.1) / Special certificates (Health / veterinary)
:::
