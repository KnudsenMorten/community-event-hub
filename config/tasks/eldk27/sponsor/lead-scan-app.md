:::section STEP 2 — BOOTH MEMBERS MUST DOWNLOAD THE LEAD SCAN APP
* Download the event platform's attendee app (for Zoho Backstage: **'Zoho Backstage for Attendees'**) from Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS).
* Open the app and tap **'Sign in'**. Enter the email address you used when you invited them.
* They will receive a temporary password (OTP) by email — paste it into the app to log in.
* Search for the event by its name. The {{editionCode}} event will appear under **'My Events'**; tap to open it, then use the **'Lead Scan'** / badge-scanner feature at the booth.
:::
