# A/V, Comfort Screen, HDMI Switches, Stage-timer

Describe the room technology speakers can expect. The sections below are a starting point — keep

## A/V

Every room is equipped with A/V, including microphones and speakers. We expect speakers to be able to
set themselves up. If you know in advance that you'll need help, let us know and we'll arrange
technical support for your session.

If something goes wrong during the event, tell the crew in the speaker chat. We'll escalate
immediately — most issues are resolved within a few minutes.

## Stage timer

If your rooms have a stage timer in front of the speaker, explain what it shows and who controls it.
A central timer helps keep sessions running to the agenda.

## HDMI switchers and comfort screen

If every room has an HDMI switcher and a comfort screen in front of the speaker, that means:

- two laptops can be connected at once, with easy switching between them,
- a much smoother experience for sessions with multiple speakers,
- seamless transitions between demo machines,
- no need to turn around to see the main screen.
