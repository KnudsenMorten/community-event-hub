## Please come early
Arriving early has many advantages: you'll be able to join the morning sessions, secure a great spot
for the keynote, avoid long check-in lines — and enjoy breakfast. State when doors open and when the
first session starts on each day.

## Cloakroom
Say whether there is a cloakroom, how much it holds, and where to leave luggage.

## Check-in
Have your ticket QR code ready for scanning. Say where attendees find it — the confirmation e-mail
or the event app.

## Ticket changes
If you need to make any changes to your ticket (such as a name update), please handle this in
advance by contacting the organizers. This helps ensure a smooth, fast check-in for everyone.

## Badge
For security reasons, wear your badge at all times inside the venue. On a multi-day event, remember
to bring it on the following days.

## Where to find the updated agenda
The latest agenda (including room details) is in the event app, on the event website, and on the
screens around the venue.

## When can I access the session presentations?
The final session presentations are published in the hub under **Sessions → Slides** shortly before
the event, so you can review topics in advance. Some speakers publish a preview earlier — it appears
at the same place.

## Session capacity
Some session rooms may reach full capacity. We recommend bookmarking alternative sessions in advance
as a backup.

## Photography & video notice {#photography-video}
We'll be capturing moments from the event with photographers and a video team. If you prefer not to
be included, please inform a member of the photo or video team.
