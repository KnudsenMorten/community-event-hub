# Session Feedback — how your audience rates your session

As a speaker, it helps to know how attendees can give feedback on your session, so you can encourage it from the stage.

Attendees can share feedback for **up to 30 minutes after your session** in two ways:
- **On-site evaluation device** — if the organizers place one by the exit: **one click** (score only). Attendees tap a face on their way out of the room.
- **QR code** — the QR code you show in your presentation lets attendees leave a **score *and* comments** — it only takes about 10 seconds. (Download your session's QR code from your Session Evaluation page / My Sessions.)

**You'll automatically receive your results by email shortly after your session finishes.**

Please encourage your audience to give feedback — a quick score if they enjoyed the talk, and the QR code for specifics.
