# Session Evaluations

**Session evaluations** — We want session evaluation to be easy and instant, right after the session, because attendees rarely prioritize giving feedback after the event. Attendees can scan the QR code shown in your presentation to leave a score and comments, and the organizers may also place a physical evaluation device in the room for a one-click score on the way out. As a speaker, you automatically get your results by email shortly after your session finishes.
