# Session Guidelines

## Audience level

Survey your attendees about their preferred topics and level, and tell speakers what you found —
for example, that many attendees are at **Expert (400)** level and want sessions to **go deeper**.

Check the [survey results](/survey/eldk27-topics/results) and align your session to the
audience's level.

## Session structure & time — leave room for questions

Tell speakers how long the slots are. Longer slots leave room for in-depth content and, in
particular, better and more engaging Q&A. Attendees value it when speakers:

- actively encourage questions,
- allow time for discussion,
- make the session as interactive as possible.

Please make sure your session fits inside the allocated slot, **including Q&A**.

> **A lesson worth passing on:** speakers who save their demos for the end sometimes never get to
> show them because of the questions along the way. Plan your demos early and leave room for the
> questions.

## Session content — more demos, fewer slides

Attendee feedback at technical community events is very consistent: **attendees love demos**. For
technical sessions (panel discussions excepted), we encourage you to:

- put a strong emphasis on demos,
- minimize slides where possible,
- focus on real-world scenarios, configurations, tools and workflows.

## Session room allocation

All rooms are allocated in advance in the agenda, where you can also see each room's capacity. Be
aware that room allocation may still change based on attendee interest.

## Speaker room

Say where speakers can prepare and fine-tune their presentation on the day.
